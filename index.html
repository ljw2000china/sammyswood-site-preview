The house

Six decades of French
savoir-faire, one
uncompromising standard.

Founded in France in 1961, Sammys was built on a simple conviction: a room should be measured, not decorated. Every commission begins with the architecture of the space — its light, its proportions, the way a family actually moves through a morning.

We design, engineer and manufacture in-house. Nothing is outsourced, nothing is approximated. The result is furniture with the quiet authority of something made once, and made properly.

The commission

How a room
comes to be.

Four stages, one dedicated team — from first conversation to final installation.

01

Consultation

We listen first: how you cook, store, entertain. Measurements, light and constraints are documented on site or from your architect's drawings.

02

Design

Elevations, material boards and 3D studies. Every detail — grain direction, reveal, hardware — is resolved before a single panel is cut.

03

Atelier

Built in our own workshop: solid timber, veneer, lacquer and stone, finished and quality-checked under one roof.

04

Installation

Delivered and fitted by our own team, with a single point of contact until the last handle is aligned.
